About Us

Building a bridge between you and the city

Mowasalat is more than just a transport app; it's your daily partner in smart mobility.

Mowasalat is a smart transport app that connects passengers with verified drivers to provide fast and safe city travel. We offer a modern experience that combines easy ride booking with flexible monthly subscription options.

Our goal is to make daily mobility easier and more reliable using modern technology and providing high-quality service for everyone. We believe every ride should be an enjoyable and safe experience.
